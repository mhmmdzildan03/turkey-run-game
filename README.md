Gobble Dash — The Great Turkey Escape: Thanksgiving is coming and you are the main course. Outrun Farmer Bram across the farmyard, through the cornfield and past the kitchen ovens, leaping fences and hay bales, sliding under swinging carving knives and dodging falling ones, grabbing corn for points and a head start, until you burst into the Freedom Forest — or get caught and served for dinner. A PixiJS runner with parallax golden-hour farm scenery, an animated turkey and farmer, particles, screen shake, near-miss slow motion, synthesized gobbles and a banjo-pluck soundtrack. Three lives, a farmer-gap meter, and a 3,000 m escape to win.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Space / ↑ / click to jump (twice for a double jump), ↓ / S to slide, Shift / → to sprint, M for sound, R to restart.
